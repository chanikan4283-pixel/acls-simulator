# ACLS sim player guide

Build: 4.0.0-preview.5-en

Choose Case 1–6 on the home screen. The selection cards deliberately omit diagnoses and rhythm names. Cases 1–5 retain the original clinical scenarios. Case 6 opens the existing ECG interpretation exercise.

## Clinical scenarios

Read the patient briefing and press **Start scenario**. The clinical timer begins at that point. Assess the patient, direct the team using the bottom action bar, and reassess after interventions or deterioration.

- **Assess:** pulse and breathing, monitor, examination, history, ECG and flat-line checks.
- **Resuscitate:** compressions, compressor changes, rhythm checks, oxygen and ventilation.
- **Electrical:** pads, energy, synchronization, charge, clear, shock and pacing.
- **Drugs / Access:** vascular access, medication, dose, unit and route.
- **Investigate:** findings, working cause and cause-directed team care.
- **Handover:** reassessment, ongoing-care targets and completion.

Guided practice includes coaching. Independent practice allows hints on request. Rhythm information may appear after monitor attachment in guided practice; the numbered selection cards and initial briefing headings do not reveal the rhythm.

## ECG practice

Case 6 asks you to interpret tracings. Choose an answer to reveal the explanation. Use the six-step guide to review rate, regularity, P waves, PR relationships, QRS complexes and clinical context.

## iPad controls

Use the Overview, Patient, Airway and Defib camera buttons. Landscape gives treatment panels more space; portrait places the monitor above the scene. Start with Standard graphics, or select Performance for reduced shadows. Reduced motion limits camera transitions.

## Timing and review

Opening the guide or reference information pauses practice time and records the pause. Treatment panels do not pause the scenario. Leaving the app pauses it automatically; press Resume when ready.

Use Finish / Review to end early, or complete the handover to finish the case. The debrief shows objectives, points, errors, timing and simulated compression fraction. Export JSON or CSV to save the session locally. Earlier-language saved reports remain in storage but are not displayed in the English interface; a new session creates an English report.

The application is an educational preview. Scripted recovery does not predict real outcomes. Game points are not a validated research knowledge measure. Physical iPad testing and expert research validation remain separate tasks.


## Direct your team
Tap a character or role label, or use the permanent team buttons. Airway opens oxygen, bag-mask ventilation, intubation and breathing reassessment. After BVM is established, Intubate starts an animated preparation/placement/confirmation sequence; advanced-airway status is recorded only when it finishes. Continue other team care while it runs. Pause or hands-clear holds the sequence. This simplified animation does not teach manual intubation technique.
